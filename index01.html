Why I Am Learning to Code

My Motivation

I started learning to code because I wanted to understand how the technology around me actually works. Every app, website, and device is built from instructions that someone wrote, and I want to be the person who writes them.

Coding is a skill that opens doors. It lets me turn ideas into working things, and I find that incredibly exciting.

What I Hope to Achieve

Short-Term Goals

Right now, my goal is to build a solid foundation in the basics.
I want to write clean code, understand how it works,
and finish small projects from start to end.

Long-Term Goals

In the long run, I want to build real, useful project and work as a developer. I know the journey will be challenging, but every bug I fix teaches me something new.

Final Thoughts

Learning to code is not just about getting a job or build an indpendent sorce of income. It is about thinking differently and solving problems step by step.
